Things I built because I wanted to know

Open source · computer vision↗

SwingTrace

Golf club tracking from ordinary swing video. A fine-tuned YOLO11m detector finds the shaft, club head and hands, then swing-path reconstruction recovers missed heads from shaft geometry and smooths the path with cubic splines. Weights on Hugging Face; trained on the CC BY 4.0 golf-club-tracking dataset from Roboflow Universe.

Club tracked in 93.4% of 3,018 frames across 7 unseen videos, vs 71.6% from head detection alone
Open source · ML systemsrepo ↗

llm-roofline-agent

A roofline performance model for LLM inference: the arithmetic intensity of prefill and decode against a GPU's compute and bandwidth ceilings, validated on a T4. Try it: decode reads every weight once per step, so at batch 1 it does about 1 FLOP per byte and sits far below the 203 FLOP/byte ridge.
